# S6: x86 CPU (plan_v3.md 8.4). The header line `kmode cpu` makes pod_env.py
# set KMODE=cpu: CPU wheels of JAX, torch and TF. The "cpu" and "any" stages
# run.
# Pod: RunPod CPU pod, cpu3c, 8 vCPU, $0.24/h (0.03 per vCPU-hour, 16 GB RAM).
# Budget 0.5 h. Session deadline 45 min.
# Times from the create (pod_env.py): KSTAGES_UNTIL 30 min, KHARD_UNTIL
# 35 min. Worst case: the stages stop at 30 min, KDONE at about 31 min, the
# grace ends at about 36 min. The watchdog can stop the pod 1 min earlier.
# Format: SESSION.md, "Session files". Stage order is priority order.
#
# The grid is smaller than the GPU grid: 3 shapes, M up to 256. A float32
# M = 2048 cell at 8192 x 8192 takes about 1 s per call on 8 vCPU. Check the
# stage times in the dry run on the measurement commit.
session S6
venvs jax-ci jax-new torch tf
install_timeout 10
global_timeout 25
deadline 45
grace_s 300
kmode cpu
l2_mb 0
